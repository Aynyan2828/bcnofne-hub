We released the theme song of our ship today: "6-gen to Boku no Fune" (Six Strings and Me). It's an acoustic-guitar-led tune, just right for setting sail.
An English-lyrics version is ready too, and we're getting it out the door.
The song now sits on the Singles shelf of the site, and the Japanese and English pages each show it in their own way. Come have a listen!
The music video for it is a work in progress, with a lot of love going into it. Please wait for it a little longer.
